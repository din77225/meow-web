What needs attention

Mobile render-blocking requests and image delivery are the main performance issues. CSP allows inline scripts for existing static pages. No high or critical production dependency advisories; one low. Entity and freshness metadata can improve. The AEO short-text flag on the link page is a heuristic, not evidence of an empty page: its links and videos are already in the HTML.

What was verified

Both tutorial feeds rebuild automatically. A future-upload fixture appeared first on both pages, including their static fallback content. The homepage retains ten videos, and the link page retains five. The scheduled Cloudflare hook exists and recent runs actually triggered builds. New cadence: every six hours after deployment, subject to scheduler delays; failures retain last good data.

The carousel shows four logos on desktop and two on mobile. Arrows, pause, requested brand order, 44px click targets and horizontal overflow were checked. Build and TypeScript checks pass.
