Round 2 Prompt adherence

The raspberry thief

You put in: One photo of you

Use your own face reference.

Create a cinematic, playful photograph in a charming sunlit kitchen. The adult woman in the reference has caught a tiny adult version of herself stealing a raspberry. The full-size woman holds the miniature by pinching the back of her cropped plum jacket, lifting her into midair above the kitchen counter. They look directly at each other: the larger woman gives a knowing raised eyebrow, the miniature looks caught red-handed and hugs a raspberry nearly as wide as her torso. Both share the reference woman's face and hairstyle, wearing fashion-forward plum jackets, ivory bow blouses, black shorts and burgundy boots. The miniature is a uniformly shrunken real adult with natural adult head-to-body proportions, not a doll or child. Clear believable grip on the fabric, dangling legs and contact shadows. Warm cream and sage kitchen, scattered raspberries, magical yet photorealistic.

Round 6 World understanding

The mirror and the closed book

You put in: One image of the front book cover

Input references

Create a bright cinematic fashion photograph of a rebellious 22-year-old woman standing in front of a large flat dressing-room mirror, holding one closed hardcover book with BOTH hands at chest height. Show her and her reflection. Her reflection must match her actual head tilt, body posture, hand positions and the book's position and closed state.

She wears a sleeveless lilac top, black jeans and silver ear piercings, with her hair pinned up. A tattoo on her LEFT upper arm reads "today is the day" in lowercase, as one continuous line along her arm from elbow toward shoulder. Keep the letters side by side, not stacked. The back of her neck has a tattoo reading "Alice".

Use the supplied image only for the exact red-and-silver FRONT book cover, preserving its title, author, silhouette and ornamentation. The front cover faces the mirror; the BACK cover faces the camera and is plain solid red with no writing or decoration. Both hands hold the book shut, with the covers flat against the page block. It is closed in both the real view and the reflection.

"MEET ME AT NINE" is written in coral lipstick directly on the mirror beside her reflection. Bright soft daylight, cream, blush and lilac colors, natural skin texture, cinematic editorial photography.
